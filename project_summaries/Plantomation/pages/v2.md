# Plantomation V2 – 12-Channel Controller with Two Pumps (ESP32-S3)

[← Back to Plantomation](../plantomation.md)

V2 scales Plantomation from 4 to **12 plants** and adds a **second pump**, so every plant can get **water and liquid fertilizer** on separate schedules. It also adds inputs for **air temperature, air humidity and sunlight**, and expansion headers for I²C and SPI devices.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Revision** | Version 1.0, schematic dated 2023-12-04 |
| **Status** | PCB design finished, not yet ordered. Firmware: hardware test only. Web pages drafted |
| **MCU** | ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (PlatformIO board: ESP32-S3 with 8 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM) |
| **Supply** | 12 V DC (JST-XH, silkscreen "DC IN") |
| **Outputs** | 12 × valve, 2 × pump (12 V, low-side), 2 status LEDs – all via shift registers |
| **Inputs** | 12 × moisture sensor + 4 auxiliary analog inputs (air temp, humidity, sunlight, spill) via 2 × MCP3208 |
| **Storage** | microSD (Hirose DM3AT, card detect) |

| **PCB** | 100 × 100 mm, **4 layers**, 4 × M3 mounting holes |
| **Firmware** | PlatformIO, Arduino framework (`V2/Firmware`) |

*Note: the V2 README says "ESP32-S3-WROVER"; the schematic and BOM use the **ESP32-S3-WROOM-1**.*

## 1. What changed from V1 – and why

| Topic | V1 | V2 | Reason *(inferred)* |
|---|---|---|---|
| Channels | 4 | 12 | V1's channel count was limited by free ESP32 pins |
| Outputs | direct GPIO | 2 × **74HC595** (16 bits over 3 pins) | Frees GPIOs, easy to extend |
| Analog inputs | ESP32 ADC1 | 2 × **MCP3208** 12-bit, 16 channels over SPI | More channels, better ADC linearity than the ESP32 ADC |
| Pumps | 1 | 2 | Water + fertilizer |
| Valve MOSFET | BSS138 | **BSS306** | More current headroom for 12 V solenoid valves |
| 5 V rail | L7805 + heatsink | **TPS56339** buck converter | Less heat, more current |
| USB | CP2102N bridge | ESP32-S3 **native USB** (CDC + JTAG) | Fewer parts, built-in debugging |
| Sensor connectors | JST-XH | 2.54 mm headers (Sig / Vcc / GND rows) | 12 sensors in a compact row |
| Environment | – | Air temp, humidity, sunlight inputs | Plant-climate monitoring |
| Expansion | – | I²C + SPI at 3.3 V and 5 V | Displays, digital sensors, more boards |

### Power

| Rail | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 12 V | DC input | Valves, pumps |
| 5 V | **TPS56339** synchronous buck, 5.6 µH Vishay IHLP-4040 inductor, 22 µF output caps | Moisture sensors, 5 V expansion ports, AMS1117 input |
| 3.3 V | **AMS1117-3.3** | ESP32-S3, logic, ADCs |
| VDDA | 3.3 V through a ferrite bead and filter caps | Analog supply for the MCP3208s |

### Outputs – shift registers and drivers

Two daisy-chained **74HC595** give 16 outputs:

| Bits | Function |
|---|---|
| 2 | Status LEDs ("State1", "State2") |
| 12 | Valves 1–12 |
| 2 | Pump 1, Pump 2 |

Each valve and pump is switched by a **BSS306** N-MOSFET (1 kΩ gate resistor, 100 kΩ pull-down, LL4148 flyback diode) and has its own indicator LED – **16 LEDs** in total. Valves connect to a **2 × 12 pin header**, pumps to two JST-XH connectors.

A 5-pin **"Shifter"** socket (3V3, CLK, Latch, Dout, GND) brings the shift-register chain out of the board – *presumably* to add more output boards later.

### Inputs – MCP3208 ADCs

Two **MCP3208** (8 channels each, 12 bit, SPI, chip selects CS0/CS1) give 16 analog inputs:

- **12 × moisture sensor** on three 12-pin rows: **Sig**, **Vcc (5 V)** and **GND**.
- **4 × auxiliary sensor**, each a 3-pin header labelled **Spillage**, **Sunlight**, **Humidity** and **Air Temp**. A solder jumper per sensor selects **3.3 V or 5 V** supply (silkscreen "3V3 / SIG / 5V"). Unpopulated resistor footprints are marked *"Optional"* in the schematic.
- Four RC filters (100 Ω / 1 nF) in the BOM – *presumably* on the four auxiliary inputs.

### Interfaces

| Interface | Details |
|---|---|
| microSD | Hirose DM3AT with card detect, SPI chip select CS2 |
| I²C | Two 4-pin headers: one at 3.3 V, one at **5 V** via BSS138 level shifters |
| SPI | Two 6-pin headers (V+, SCK, MISO, MOSI, CS, GND): CS3 at 3.3 V, CS4 at **5 V** via BSS138 level shifters |
| USB | USB-B to the ESP32-S3 native USB, USBLC6-2SC6 ESD protection, 22 Ω series resistors |
| Buttons | **RESET**, **Boot0** |
| UART1 + DIR | Net labels `TxD_1`, `RxD_1`, `DIR` exist on GPIO17/18/38 but are not routed to a connector – *reserved* |

## 3. Firmware (current state)

The V2 firmware is a **hardware bring-up test**:

- A `channels` class holds the 12 valve bits, two pump bits and two status bits and shifts them out as 16 bits (`set_ch()`, `set_pump()`, `set_state()`, `update()`).
- The loop steps through channels **1 to 12**. Each channel opens for 1 s together with pump 1 or pump 2 (alternating), then everything is off for 1 s.

### Build environments

| Environment | Use |
|---|---|
| `esp32dev` | Upload over native USB (`/dev/ttyACM*`), 921600 baud – preferred |
| `esp32ota` | Upload over the air |
| `esp32dev-debug` | Experimental debugging with the ESP32-S3 built-in USB-JTAG |

A custom board file `boards/esp32-s3-devkit-1-n8r8.json` defines 8 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM and USB-CDC on boot.

### Web pages (drafted)

The V2 HTML pages are ready for 12 channels:

- **Control:** one card per channel with plant name, **interval and volume for pump 1 and pump 2**, moisture threshold and current moisture.
- **Configuration:** mode table for 12 channels (**Disabled / Time / Moisture**), **two pump rates**, log and debug level, Wi-Fi settings, and a **Sensor Calibration** section (*"please have the sensor of Channel 1 as well as dry and wet medium at hand"*). A note warns that an access-point password needs **at least 8 characters**.

*Note: in V2 the mode numbers change – `1` = Time, `2` = Moisture (V1: `1` = Moisture, `2` = Time).*
