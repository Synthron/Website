Amateur Radio

All my Amateur Radio projects and topics.

I passed my amateur radio exam in May 2026 and received my callsign DM7WB.

DM stands for Germany and Marco, the 7 fits my favorite number and license class, WB stands for Weingarten (Baden).

I started with handheld radios in the 2m/70cm band. With the local repeaters, I gained my first QSO experiences and gradually got more involved.

With the QMX+ from QRP-Labs, the first shortwave transceiver was added, but the first attempts were limited to FT8 due to lack of experience.

Thanks to my friend Christian DC2SV, who lent me his Icom transceivers for some time, I was able to gain much more experience and QSOs, and also enjoy 100W transmit power. This opened up many new possibilities.

After joining the DARC through the local club A36, I made many new contacts. Among other things, the first VHF contests were held at the club shack.

Over time, I have tried and (re)built many things. To document this more openly, all the projects that actually came to fruition are listed here.

Have fun browsing. From modifications to reproductions and developments, everything is included here.
