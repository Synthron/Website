The Idea

A fellow club member has the QMX from QRP-Labs and wanted a small and compact microphone for it. Since I also have a QMX+, he came to me and asked if we could recreate the microphone capsule linked by QRP Labs.

So I downloaded the STL files and modified the case so that our respective callsigns are layed in the print, while my colleague ordered the hardware for it.

2. Case and Electronics

The microphone itself is very simple. Two 3D-printed shells enclose a jack, a push button, and the electret capsule.

The only important thing was to find the right capsule. The original link specifies a capsule from Digikey, but since it was relatively expensive at around €4, we looked for an alternative capsule with the same internal resistance. Our capsule has slightly more gain but less SNR.

I myself have not used the microphone for a QSO yet, but via the QMX+ terminal I could see that there is a clear response when speaking. My colleague took the microphone on a trip and was able to make some QSOs with it, so the little gadget seems to work well enough.

Conclusion

For what is inside the microphone, it seems to work very well. Price-wise, it is almost unbeatable. With the original capsule, the material costs would be around €8, but we were able to reduce this to around €3-4 with a cheaper electret capsule.

In my opinion, it is worth making, especially if you have to work with a very tight budget.
