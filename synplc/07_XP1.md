# XP1 – PWM Power-Driver Prototype (`XP_V1`) – optional sub-page source

> Not part of the six released V2 modules. Publish only as "in development / prototype", e.g. under a "Roadmap" section or as a short extra sub-page. Basis for the **planned PWM module** (type bits 100, `DEV_PWM 0x80`).

## Hardware (verified in `hardware/pcbs/XP_V1/XP_V1.sch`)
- **PCA9635** 16-channel, 8-bit I²C PWM/LED driver (TSSOP-28) as the PWM source.
- 8 PWM channels buffered with BC846 (NPN) / BC856 (PNP) transistor stages, 1 kΩ gate resistors.
- **8 open-drain output stages** with **BUK98150-55A** logic-level N-channel MOSFETs (55 V).
- 5 V logic supply (+5 V / GND1). No microcontroller, no bus transceivers and no address switch on this board – it is a *driven* board; PWM setpoints would come from a controller over I²C.
- The schematic is partial (unannotated references `U?`, `Q?`) – treat all details as preliminary.

## Software
None. A future PWM module is expected to follow the common RS485 register scheme (per-channel duty setpoints, enable, status, error).
